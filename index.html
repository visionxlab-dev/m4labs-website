What We Sell

Three Mortgage Lead Products

Purchase Mortgage Leads

Homebuyers actively shopping for a purchase loan, screened on credit, household income and purchase price before they reach you.

Cash-Out Refinance Leads

Homeowners with equity who want to take cash out through a refinance, screened on credit, home value, cash-out amount and loan-to-value.

DSCR Investor Leads

Investors buying or refinancing rental property, qualified on the property and credit rather than on household income.

The exact criteria for each product are published at visionxlab.com.

How It Works

One Lead, One Lending Team

A borrower answers our ad, completes a short form on our own landing page, and is checked against the criteria for the product they asked about. A lead that misses them is never delivered and never billed. One that passes goes into your CRM in real time, and to nobody else.

  • Our own advertising and landing pages
  • Screened against fixed criteria before delivery
  • Exclusive leads, never resold, never shared
  • TCPA consent documented on every lead
  • Pay per qualified lead, no binding contract
